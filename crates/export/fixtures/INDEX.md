## Ready-to-print demos

### `print_in_place_cam_bolt_*.3mf` (preferred complex demo)

Four-body print-in-place **cam bolt** (no supports):

- Black housing + red bolt + blue follower + orange dial
- Slide bolt (Y) → stepped wedge drives follower (+X) → twist dial to lock
- Every inter-body box pair ≥ 0.4 mm clearance
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 15% infill, no supports, PLA
- MCP: `demo_export_pip_3mf` with `kind: "cam_bolt"` (default)

### `print_in_place_clip_*.3mf`

Three-body captive drawer clip (simpler PIP):

- Black housing + red drawer + blue latch
- MCP: `demo_export_pip_3mf` with `kind: "clip"`

Legacy alias files `print_in_place_latch_*.3mf` match the clip geometry.

Also includes simple `cube_*.3mf` geometry checks.

Do not hand-edit `smoke/*.3mf`; re-run the fixture test after writer or demo-mesh changes.
