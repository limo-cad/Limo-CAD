# Community conduct

Limo CAD welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Discuss the work respectfully, give specific and constructive
feedback, and respect people's privacy and intellectual property.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, publication of
private information and repeated disruption are unacceptable in this project's
issues, reviews, discussions and other community spaces.

Maintainers `@jackControls` and `@jeffglousher` may edit or remove disruptive
content, ask participants to change their behavior, or restrict participation
when needed. Apply these expectations consistently, including to maintainers.
Report abusive content using GitHub's **Report content** menu so it can be
reviewed privately. Report software vulnerabilities through
[the security policy](SECURITY.md).
