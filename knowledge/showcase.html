Printed vise

100 mm jaws, 90 mm travel and a custom 24 × 4 mm rounded screw with a shallow print flat.

Watch the jaw follow the screw, inspect the captured guides and read the carriage drawing. The editable model includes seven drawing sheets. Six printed parts plus M5/M6 hardware; optional mounting hardware is shown in assembly context.

Vertical-axis turbine
