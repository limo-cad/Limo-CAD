1. The line runs from (12,20) to (28,20); the loose point begins at (18,24).

2. Select the loose point and then the line, not one of its endpoints.

3. Choose MidPoint to bind the point to the midpoint of the full line.

4. The point follows the line's midpoint; moving a line endpoint later preserves the halfway relationship.
