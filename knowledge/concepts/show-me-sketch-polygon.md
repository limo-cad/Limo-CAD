1. Choose Polygon and place its first center at (20,16).

2. Use Inscribed, 6 sides, radius 5 and rotation 0°; confirm the lower hexagon.

3. Place a second center at (20,30). Switch to Circumscribed with the same 6 sides, radius 5 and rotation 0°.

4. The upper hexagon's sides are 5 mm from its center; the lower hexagon's vertices are 5 mm from its center.
