title: Add a diagonal bracket rib
description: Thicken a prepared diagonal centerline into a 3 mm rib joined to both bracket legs.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23

---

1. Select the prepared diagonal centerline between the base and upright at Y20.

2. Preview a 2 mm thick rib with 4 mm depth symmetric about its sketch plane.

3. Increase thickness to 3 mm and keep Join as the operation.

4. Apply Rib. Both ends overlap the bracket, leaving one connected body.
