1. Choose Create Sketch and the XZ plane. Draw one connected, closed L-profile.

2. Constrain the outer dimensions to 32 × 44 mm and the wall thickness to 8 mm. Keep all six edges connected.

3. Finish the sketch. Extrude uses this exact profile in the next example.
