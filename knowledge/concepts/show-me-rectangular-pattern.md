title: Repeat brackets in two directions
description: Make a 3 × 2 body pattern from one prepared bracket with clear spacing in X and Y.

1. Select the prepared bracket as the pattern source.

2. Preview three instances along X at 48 mm pitch, including the source.

3. Add two rows along Y at 56 mm pitch to create six brackets in total.

4. Apply Rectangular pattern. Each instance remains a separate body.
