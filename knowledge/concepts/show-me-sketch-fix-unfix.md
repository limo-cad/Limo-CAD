1. The R4 circle at (20,20) is unconstrained. Select its circular boundary.

2. Choose Fix/UnFix once to lock the circle at its current position and size.

3. The fixed snapshot shows fully defined geometry. Select the same circle and choose Fix/UnFix again.

4. The circle stays at its last position but is free to edit again; its Fix relationship has been removed.
