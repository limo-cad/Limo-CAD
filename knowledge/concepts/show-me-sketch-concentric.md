1. The R3 circle starts at (19,22); the R6 clearance circle starts at (22,24).

2. Select both circles. Their radii should remain different.

3. Choose Concentric to bring their centers together without making their sizes equal.

4. The circles share a center and retain radii 3 and 6 mm, forming a uniform clearance ring.
