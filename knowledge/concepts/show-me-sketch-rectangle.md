1. Use 2-Point Rectangle in the bracket's open layout area.

2. Click (12,12), then point toward (28,20). Enter width 16 and height 8.

3. The lower rectangle is complete. Switch to Center Rectangle; click center (20,30), then aim toward (28,34).

4. Enter full width 16 and full height 8 and commit. Both rectangles are 16 × 8 mm; the second is centered.
