title: Sketch Scale
description: Enlarge a clearance circle around its own center by a factor of 1.5.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, sketch, sketch_scale
keywords: sketch scale, show-me-sketch-edit
related_recipes: show-me-sketch-edit
---

1. Choose Sketch Scale and select the R3 circle at (20,20).

2. Enter Origin X 20 and Origin Y 20 so the center stays in place.

3. Type 1.5 as the unitless factor; the radius will increase from 3 to 4.5 mm.

4. Apply the scale. One R4.5 circle remains centered at (20,20).
