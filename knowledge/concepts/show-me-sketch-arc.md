1. Place the center at (20,18) in the bracket layout area.

2. Pick start (26,18), enter radius 6, then sweep counterclockwise to (20,24) through 90°.

3. With the quarter arc complete, choose 3-Point Arc: click start (14,30), on-arc point (20,36), then end (26,30).

4. The committed geometry contains an R6 quarter arc and an R6 upper semicircle. Pick order controls the arc side.
