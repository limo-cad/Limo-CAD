1. The XZ bracket outline has five edges. Choose Line to close the left side.

2. Click the existing endpoint at (0,44), rather than nearby empty space.

3. Click (0,0). The two existing endpoints define a 44 mm vertical segment.

4. Press Escape to end the chain. Six connected edges now enclose the bracket section.
