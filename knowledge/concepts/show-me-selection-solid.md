title: Select a face or an edge
description: Distinguish the bracket's upright face from its top edge before starting another tool.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, selection_solid
keywords: selection solid, show-me-selection-solid
related_recipes: show-me-selection-solid
---

1. Choose Select in the Solid workspace and inspect the prepared bracket.

2. Click inside the upright's inner face. The face highlight identifies a surface selection.

3. Click the top edge of that face. The edge highlight identifies a curve selection.

4. Click empty space to clear the selection. Selecting geometry does not create or modify a feature.
