1. Choose Offset and select the R4 circle centered at (20,20).

2. Click (27,20), outside the circle; this pick determines the offset side.

3. Type distance 2. The outward result should have radius 6 mm.

4. Apply the offset. The original R4 and new R6 circles share a center and leave a 2 mm gap.
