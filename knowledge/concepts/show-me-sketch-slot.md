1. Choose a straight Slot mode; rows at y=14, 24 and 34 keep the examples separate.

2. Click cap centers (16,14) and (24,14), then width point (20,16); enter width 4.

3. The first slot is complete. In Overall mode click (14,24), (26,24), then width point (20,26).

4. In Center Point mode click (20,34), cap center (24,34), then (20,36) for width 4. All three slots are 12 × 4 mm.
