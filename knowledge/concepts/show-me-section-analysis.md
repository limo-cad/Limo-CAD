1. Open Section analysis and choose the prepared bracket body.

2. Choose XZ at Y = 20 mm. The capped cutaway exposes the L section halfway across the 40 mm width.

3. Reverse the retained side. A probe at Z = 20 mm crosses 8 mm of upright material in either view.

4. Close Section analysis to restore the complete body. The original geometry and history stay intact.
