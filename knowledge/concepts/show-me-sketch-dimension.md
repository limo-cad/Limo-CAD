title: Sketch Dimension
description: Drive a layout line from 10 to 16 mm while keeping its left endpoint anchored.

updated: 2026-10-23

1. Choose Sketch Dimension for the horizontal line from (12,16) to (22,16). Its left endpoint is fixed.

2. Click the line, then place its dimension text below it near (20,12).

3. Type 16 mm. This changes the line length rather than adding a reference measurement.

4. Confirm the value. The right endpoint moves to (28,16), and the dimension reads 16 mm.
