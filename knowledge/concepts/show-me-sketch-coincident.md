1. The point near (18,22) is above the horizontal carrier at y=20.

2. Select the point, then the horizontal line from (12,20) to (28,20).

3. Choose Coincident to put the point on the line; a single point-on-line relation is sufficient.

4. The point now lies on the carrier. The relationship remains editable and does not fix its position along the line.
