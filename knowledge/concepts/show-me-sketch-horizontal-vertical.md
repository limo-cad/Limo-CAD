1. The lower line is nearly horizontal; the upper line is nearly vertical.

2. Select only the lower line and choose Horizontal/Vertical to make it horizontal.

3. Clear the first selection. Select the upper line and choose Horizontal/Vertical to make it vertical.

4. Both lines now follow the sketch axes, with one horizontal and one vertical relationship.
