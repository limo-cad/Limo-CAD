1. Choose Center Diameter Circle on the upright wall.

2. Click (4,14), aim toward (6,14), type diameter 4 and confirm.

3. For 2-Point Circle, click (2,28), then aim at (6,28); these picks span the diameter.

4. Confirm diameter 4. Both circles have radius 2 mm, with centers at (4,14) and (4,28).
