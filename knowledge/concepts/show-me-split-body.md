1. Select the prepared 40 mm wide bracket.

2. Choose the prepared construction plane through Y20 as the splitting reference.

3. Preview the split through the base and upright, producing two 20 mm wide pieces.

4. Apply Split body. The two bodies still touch at the plane; no gap or extra cut is introduced.
