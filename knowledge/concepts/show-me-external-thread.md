1. Select the cylindrical side of the prepared 8 mm diameter, 14 mm long pin.

2. Choose ISO metric coarse M8 × 1.25, class 6g, right hand, and preview 5 mm depth.

3. Set 10 mm threaded depth with Modeled representation to create real helical geometry.

4. Apply External thread. The remaining shaft length provides an unthreaded shoulder.
