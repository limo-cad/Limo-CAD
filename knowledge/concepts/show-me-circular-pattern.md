1. Select the prepared 8 × 6 × 8 mm lug centered at X20/Y0.

2. Choose the world Z axis and preview two instances around a full 360° circle.

3. Set the total count to four for 90° spacing at a 20 mm radius.

4. Apply Circular pattern. The source and three copies stay separate.
