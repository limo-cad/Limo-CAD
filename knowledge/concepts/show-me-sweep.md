1. Use the prepared 4 mm circular section and two connected R20 path arcs.

2. Sweep along the first arc to preview a quarter-turn rod.

3. Include the second arc in order to complete the semicircular path.

4. Apply Sweep with corrected Frenet orientation and round-corner transition.
