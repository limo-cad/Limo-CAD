---
type: Concept
title: Offset a plane above the bracket base
description: Create a reference plane 12 mm above the prepared bracket base without changing the solid.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, offset_plane
keywords: offset plane, show-me-offset-plane
related_recipes: show-me-offset-plane
---

1. Use the prepared bracket with its base top at Z8.

2. Select the planar top face as the reference; the rectangle marks its plane.

3. Set a +12 mm offset to locate the new plane at Z20.

4. Apply Offset plane. The raised rectangle marks the datum; the bracket geometry stays unchanged.
