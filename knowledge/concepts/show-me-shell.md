1. Select the prepared 32 × 24 × 12 mm tray blank.

2. Remove its top face and preview a 1 mm inward shell.

3. Set 2 mm thickness to retain the outer dimensions and raise the inner floor to Z2.

4. Apply Shell. The tray stays open at the top with one connected solid wall.
