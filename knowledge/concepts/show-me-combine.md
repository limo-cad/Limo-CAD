title: Join an extended bracket foot
description: Fuse an overlapping foot extension into the prepared bracket to leave one connected solid.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, combine
keywords: combine, show-me-combine
related_recipes: show-me-combine
---

1. Use the prepared bracket and the separate 20 × 16 × 8 mm foot extension.

2. Select the bracket as target and the overlapping extension as tool.

3. Choose Join and turn Keep tools off so the extension is consumed.

4. Apply Combine. The completed foot reaches X44 and belongs to one solid.
