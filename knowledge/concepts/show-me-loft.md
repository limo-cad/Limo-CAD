1. Select the prepared 20 × 16 mm lower section at Z0.

2. Connect it to the 16 × 12 mm middle section at Z12.

3. Add the 10 × 8 mm upper section at Z24, keeping the sections in order.

4. Apply a ruled Loft to create one solid with planar side panels.
