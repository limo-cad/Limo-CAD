1. Choose Mirror. A circle lies at (14,20); the axis line runs vertically through x=20.

2. Click the circle first, then the vertical axis last. The axis itself is not copied.

3. Use the line from (20,10) to (20,36) as the axis, then apply Mirror.

4. The original at (14,20) remains and an equal circle appears at (26,20).
