1. Choose Trim. A horizontal line from (12,20) to (28,20) crosses boundaries at x=16 and x=24.

2. Keep both outer ends; target the horizontal segment between the two crossings.

3. Click (20,20). Trim uses the surrounding intersections as its limits.

4. The span x=16…24 is removed. Segments x=12…16 and x=24…28 remain; both crossing lines stay.
