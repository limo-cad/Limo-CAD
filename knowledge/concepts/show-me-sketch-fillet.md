1. Choose Fillet at the layout corner (14,14).

2. Click the horizontal leg toward (28,14), then the vertical leg toward (14,32).

3. Set radius 4 mm; both legs are long enough for the tangent points.

4. Apply the fillet. An R4 arc replaces the sharp corner and joins both shortened legs tangentially.
