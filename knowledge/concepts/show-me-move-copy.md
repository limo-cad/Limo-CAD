title: Copy and turn a bracket
description: Keep the prepared bracket and place a second body with a translation and 90° rotation.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, move_copy
keywords: move copy, show-me-move-copy
related_recipes: show-me-move-copy
---

1. Select the prepared bracket and enable Copy.

2. Preview a 48 mm translation along X with no rotation.

3. Set X translation to 56 mm and rotate 90° about Z around pivot X16/Y20/Z0.

4. Apply Move/Copy. The original stays in place and the turned copy remains a separate body.
