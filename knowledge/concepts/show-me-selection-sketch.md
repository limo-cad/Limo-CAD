1. Choose Select in the Sketch workspace. The prepared L section stays unchanged.

2. Click the middle of the 32 mm bottom segment to select the segment rather than an endpoint.

3. Shift-click the adjacent 8 mm vertical segment to add it to the selection.

4. Click empty space without Shift to clear both selections. Dimensions and constraints stay unchanged.
