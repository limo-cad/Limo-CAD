1. Select the prepared 5 × 12 mm section, located 5 mm from the Y axis.

2. Choose Y as the axis and preview a 120° turn.

3. Set 360° for a complete spacer with a 20 mm outside diameter.

4. Apply Revolve. The 10 mm bore stays open through the 12 mm length.
