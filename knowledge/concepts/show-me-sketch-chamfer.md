1. Choose Chamfer at the layout corner (14,14).

2. Select the horizontal leg toward (28,14), then the vertical leg toward (14,32).

3. Set the distance to 4 mm. Leave the angle blank for equal setbacks.

4. Apply the chamfer. A diagonal line joins (18,14) to (14,18), replacing the sharp corner.
