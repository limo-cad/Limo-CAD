1. The lower rail is horizontal and the upper rail is slightly tilted.

2. Select the lines (12,14)→(28,14) and (12,24)→(28,27).

3. Choose Parallel; the rails may rotate to satisfy the relation without being joined.

4. Both rails now have the same direction. Parallel alone does not set their spacing.
