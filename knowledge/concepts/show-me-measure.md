title: Measure the upright wall
description: Measure the exact gap between the upright's outside and inside faces.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, measure
keywords: measure, show-me-measure
related_recipes: show-me-measure
---

1. Open Measure on the prepared bracket.

2. Choose the outer upright face at X = 0 mm as the first selection.

3. Choose the inner upright face at X = 8 mm. Read the native minimum distance: 8 mm.

4. Close Measure. The readout is temporary and the bracket geometry stays unchanged.
