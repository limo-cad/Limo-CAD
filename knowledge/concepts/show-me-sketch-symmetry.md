1. Two points start at (14,20) and (25,22); the centerline lies at x=20.

2. Select both points, then select the vertical line from (20,12) to (20,34) last.

3. Choose Symmetry. The selected line acts as the axis while the points adjust to opposite sides.

4. The points have equal distance from the axis and the same height. The centerline remains the reference.
