1. Choose Extend. The horizontal line ends at (20,20); a vertical boundary stands at x=26.

2. Target the right endpoint, away from the bracket wall on the left.

3. Click (20,20). The line grows toward the first boundary at (26,20).

4. The endpoint now meets the boundary, with a persistent coincident relationship.
