1. The circle at (16,20) has radius 2; the circle at (24,28) has radius 3.

2. Select the two circular boundaries, not their centers.

3. Choose Equal. The solver adjusts their radii to a shared size.

4. The circles remain separate but have equal radii. A later size change can drive both through this relation.
