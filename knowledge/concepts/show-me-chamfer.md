title: Bevel the bracket top rim
description: Create flat 1 mm bevels on the four edges around the upright top face.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, chamfer
keywords: chamfer, show-me-chamfer
related_recipes: show-me-chamfer
---

1. Use the prepared bracket and locate the top of its upright at Z44.

2. Select only the four edges around that top face.

3. Set an equal-distance 1 mm chamfer and leave tangent-chain expansion off.

4. Apply Chamfer and inspect the flat bevels around the retained top face.
