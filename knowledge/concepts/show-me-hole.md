1. Select the flat top of the prepared bracket base and centers at X24/Y12 and X24/Y28.

2. Preview two 5 mm diameter blind holes with 3 mm depth.

3. Set 7 mm diameter and Through all to pass through the 8 mm base.

4. Apply Hole. Both bores belong to one editable Hole feature.
