1. The rounded corners and two 7 mm base holes are already complete. Start with the prepared 26 mm circle on the upright face; Sketch explains drawing it.

2. Choose Extrude, select the circular profile and set Operation to Subtract. Point the cut into the wall. Through All makes a through hole; Distance of 4 mm makes a blind cut in the 8 mm wall.

3. Apply and inspect the result. A through hole opens on both faces; the measured cut leaves 4 mm of material behind it. Switch the example choice to compare. The sketches, extrusions and fillets remain editable.
