1. The R4 circle at (20,21) is close to the horizontal line at y=16.

2. Select the horizontal line and the nearby circle; do not select a circle center point.

3. Choose Tangent. The solver brings the circular boundary into tangential contact with the line.

4. The line and circle meet without crossing at the tangency point, and a tangent relationship remains.
