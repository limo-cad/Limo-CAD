---
type: Concept
title: Mirror a bracket across YZ
description: Create an opposite-hand bracket across the YZ plane while retaining the prepared source.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, mirror
keywords: mirror, show-me-mirror
related_recipes: show-me-mirror
---

1. Select the prepared bracket, whose nearest side is 8 mm from YZ.

2. Choose the YZ origin plane as the mirror reference.

3. Preview the reflected copy on the opposite side, with a 16 mm gap between bodies.

4. Apply Mirror. The source and reflected bracket remain separate bodies.
