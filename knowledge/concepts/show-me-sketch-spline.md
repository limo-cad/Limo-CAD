1. Use the open area inside the bracket's L-shaped envelope.

2. Click (12,12), then (16,24); each pick lies on the resulting curve.

3. Click (24,28), then (28,36). Press Enter to finish the spline.

4. One smooth spline passes through all four points. It remains an open layout curve.
