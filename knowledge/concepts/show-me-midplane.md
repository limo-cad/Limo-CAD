title: Locate the bracket mid-width plane
description: Create a plane halfway between the prepared bracket end faces at Y0 and Y40.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, midplane
keywords: midplane, show-me-midplane
related_recipes: show-me-midplane

1. Use the prepared bracket with parallel end faces at Y0 and Y40.

2. Select those two end faces as the references.

3. Preview the midplane at Y20, halfway across the 40 mm width.

4. Apply Midplane. The rectangle marks the reference plane; the solid remains unchanged.
