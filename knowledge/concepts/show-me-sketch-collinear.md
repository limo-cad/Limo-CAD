1. The first segment runs along y=16; the second is offset and slightly tilted.

2. Select (12,16)→(18,16) and (22,18)→(28,19).

3. Choose Collinear to align both direction and lateral position.

4. Both segments lie on the same straight carrier. Their gap remains; Collinear does not join endpoints.
