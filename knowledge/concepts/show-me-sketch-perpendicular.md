1. One line follows the bracket base direction; the other is nearly upright.

2. Select (12,14)→(28,14), then (16,20)→(18,34).

3. Choose Perpendicular to constrain their directions to 90°.

4. The lines are perpendicular even though they do not share an endpoint; their lengths remain independently editable.
