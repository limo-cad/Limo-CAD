type: Concept
title: Show and hide construction references
description: Clear the bracket view without removing its saved sketch or center datum.
status: stable
updated: 2026-10-23
topics: show-me, construction_visibility
keywords: construction visibility, show-me-construction-visibility
related_recipes: show-me-construction-visibility
---

1. Find the retained L section sketch and center datum on the prepared bracket.

2. Use Show refs to hide the retained sketches and datums. The solid remains visible.

3. In the Browser, show just the section sketch. The datum stays hidden.

4. Show all construction references again. Visibility changes leave the body geometry intact.
