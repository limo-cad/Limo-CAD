1. Select the prepared straight lower front edge, which lies on XY.

2. Choose the XY origin plane as the reference; the rectangle marks its orientation.

3. Set a 30° rotation around the selected edge and inspect the tilted reference rectangle.

4. Apply Plane at angle. A new datum is created while the bracket stays unchanged.
