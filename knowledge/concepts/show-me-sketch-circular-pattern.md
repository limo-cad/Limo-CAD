1. Choose Circular Pattern and select the Ø3 circle centered at (26,24).

2. Enter center X 20 and Y 24; the source lies 6 mm from this center.

3. Set count 3 and total angle 180° and apply. Undo this trial before returning to the original.

4. Set count 4 and total angle 360° and apply. Four circles lie 90° apart, with no duplicate at the start.
